Figure 2: Persistent support evidence and SSCA-centered calibration in PULSE. In Stage I, HED
is fine-tuned on the target support set, and the resulting encoder snapshot is frozen to construct the
evidence bank E. In Stage II, the query detector is initialized with the Stage I weights and produces
query features Q, base class logits S, and predicted boxes B. SSCA matches Q with the cached
evidence E to obtain support agreement s and refines the base logits as S′ = S + G ⊙ ∆, while the
box coordinates bypass score calibration and remain unchanged. The evidence bank remains fixed
throughout Stage II training and inference. Flames indicate trainable parameters during training,
whereas snowflakes indicate frozen modules or fixed evidence. All model parameters are fixed during
inference. Test images from the DeepFish dataset (Fu et al., 2024) are adopted for illustration and do
not correspond to the sampled adaptation episode; the displayed output box is schematic rather than
an actual model prediction.
