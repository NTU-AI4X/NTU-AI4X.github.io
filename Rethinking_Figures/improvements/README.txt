Original figures and study-generated derivative figures

Scope: the original collection of 200 papers and 371 M/F figures. Each derivative is shown directly below its source figure and the source's unchanged six-dimensional scores in sample_library/index.html.

The derivative is generated by this study, not supplied by the original paper authors. The image-generation interface has not disclosed its actual backend version; these outputs are not asserted to be Image 2 outputs. Generated figures must preserve the scientific objects, relations, labels and scope of the source. Content fidelity is checked separately from visual quality.

Display states: pending; generating; failed; awaiting fidelity review; fidelity rejected; ready for independent judging; scoring; scored. Empty states do not display placeholder images. Failed/rejected assets may remain visible as documented attempts, explicitly labeled. A rejected derivative is not treated as a valid quality-evaluation outcome.

Scores are authentic independent requests under the unchanged original six-dimensional rubric, model settings, full-image and deterministic-detail-view policy, with three repeats. The gallery reports each dimension separately as median, min–max and valid n/3. Null means unable to judge, never zero. No composite score, preset five, guaranteed improvement or inference of human preference is provided. Every run's visual evidence and limitations remain accessible.

update_gallery.py reads registry.json and judging/scores.json, and only publishes scores whose derivative SHA256 matches the current image. Missing or stale score mappings are withheld. install_gallery.py only adds marked CSS/script includes to the main index; its first invocation saves the source HTML under html_backups/. Both operations are repeatable. Original score files and source images are read-only inputs.

The 160-paper main sample and 40-paper AI-style contrast remain separate. Earlier full-score exemplar searching is superseded by this derivative study; all prior candidates and actual scores remain available for audit. Superseding a search is not a claim that its original target was reached.

Final delivery, 2026-11-01: all 371 figures have been processed. 369 final derivatives covering 200 papers passed fidelity review and received three independent ratings each (1,107 current valid requests); two final fidelity rejections are documented without quality ratings. There are no pending generation, fidelity, or scoring items. One derivative, AccessWorld framework (AI_uLkUAjKWe6_F), has all six actual medians equal to 5. This is not a guaranteed all-five collection. Some interventions retain the complete original scientific panel and add source-supported explanatory keys; they are annotation/layout interventions, not complete redrawing. See completion_report.txt and judging/completion_summary.json.
