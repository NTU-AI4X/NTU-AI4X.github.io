Figure 1. Typical diffusion processes. (a) Standard diffusion maps high-quality images to Gaussian noise domain. (b) Mean-reverting diffusion drives terminal state toward a low-quality domain with stationary noise. (c) Diffusion bridge establishes direct probabilistic transitions between known distributions. All inject noise globally, disrupting overall structures and constraining transitions. (d) In contrast, our RDBM selectively reconstructs degraded regions (e.g., the doll) while preserving intact areas (e.g., the background).
