Figure 3. Overview of CUPID’s pipeline. From a single input image, CUPID first generates an occupancy and a UV cube in canonical
space. Then, a Perspective-n-Point (PnP) solver (i.e., Eq. 2) recovers the camera pose. Using this recovered camera pose, we extract pose-
